Timeline

Currently, I am a Ph.D. student at George Washington University , advised by Prof. Peng Wei . My current research focuses on machine learning and autonomous systems for air traffic control, including multi-agent trajectory modeling, generative models, and learning-based decision making.

Before joining GWU, I received my M.S. in Air Transportation from Korea Aerospace University , where I was advised by Prof. Keumjin Lee . During my master's studies, I worked on multi-agent aircraft trajectory prediction, probabilistic prediction, generative modeling, explainable machine learning, and automatic speech recognition for air traffic control. My research increasingly focused on learning representations of interactions among multiple aircraft and using them to understand and predict complex air traffic situations.

I also received my B.S. in Air Transportation from Korea Aerospace University . As an undergraduate, I studied air traffic control and completed professional air traffic controller training. I later obtained an Air Traffic Controller License from the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport of the Republic of Korea. My background in air traffic control has strongly shaped my research interests in building learning-based systems that can understand, predict, and ultimately support decision making in complex air traffic environments.

Research

My research focuses on intelligent and autonomous systems for air traffic management. I am particularly interested in learning representations of multi-agent air traffic, predicting and generating aircraft trajectories, and developing reliable decision-making systems for future autonomous air traffic control.

Multi-Agent Inverted Transformer for Flight Trajectory Prediction
Seokbin Yoon , Keumjin Lee
IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems , 2026
paper | doi

We introduce MAIFormer, a multi-agent trajectory prediction model that jointly captures each aircraft's spatio-temporal behavior and interactions among aircraft through multivariate and agent attention.

Airport Terminal Passenger Queue Forecasting for Departure Gates and Security Checkpoints
Juhwan Lee, Seokbin Yoon , Keumjin Lee , Hojong Baik, Seyeon Jung
AIAA DATC/IEEE Digital Avionics Systems Conference (DASC) , 2026
Best Paper of Session Award
arXiv

We develop a Transformer-based framework for forecasting passenger queues across airport facilities by jointly learning temporal dependencies and correlations among check-in islands, departure gates, and security checkpoints.

Probabilistic Multi-Agent Aircraft Landing Time Prediction
Kyungmin Kim, Seokbin Yoon , Keumjin Lee
AIAA SciTech , 2026
Best Student Paper Award in Intelligent Systems
paper | doi

We model aircraft landing times probabilistically while accounting for interactions among multiple aircraft, enabling both accurate predictions and explicit uncertainty estimation.

Learning to Explain Air Traffic Situation
Hong-ah Chai, Seokbin Yoon , Keumjin Lee
US-Europe Air Transportation Research & Development Symposium , 2025
arXiv

We investigate how attention learned from multi-agent aircraft trajectories can identify influential aircraft and provide interpretable representations of complex air traffic situations.

Aircraft Trajectory Dataset Augmentation in Latent Space
Seokbin Yoon , Keumjin Lee
International Journal of Aeronautical and Space Sciences , 2025
paper

We propose a latent-space aircraft trajectory augmentation framework that learns compact representations of flight trajectories and generates realistic synthetic trajectories while preserving the underlying structure of the original data.

Aircraft Trajectory Prediction with Inverted Transformer
Seokbin Yoon , Keumjin Lee
IEEE Access , 2025
paper | doi

We reformulate aircraft trajectory prediction using an inverted Transformer architecture in which trajectory variables are represented as tokens, allowing the model to explicitly learn multivariate correlations in aircraft motion.

Urban Air Mobility Fleet Rebalancing with Real-Time Updates on Estimated Time of Arrival
Jungu Kang, Seokbin Yoon , Keumjin Lee , Sang Hyun Kim
AIAA DATC/IEEE 43rd Digital Avionics Systems Conference (DASC) , 2024
Best Paper of Session Award
paper

We study real-time fleet rebalancing for urban air mobility systems using dynamically updated estimated arrival times to improve vehicle allocation and operational efficiency.

Improving Aircraft Trajectory Prediction Accuracy with Over-sampling Technique
Seokbin Yoon , Keumjin Lee
IEEE/AIAA 42nd Digital Avionics Systems Conference (DASC) , 2023
paper

We investigate trajectory data over-sampling as a method for improving learning-based aircraft trajectory prediction, particularly when representative training trajectories are limited.

Safety and Capacity Analysis Framework for Integrated UAM Operation in Airports
Naomi Hani Gray, Suyoung Shin, Seokbin Yoon , Yulim Park, Hongah Chai, Keumjin Lee , Han-Joong Kim, Se-Yeon Jung
IEEE/AIAA 42nd Digital Avionics Systems Conference (DASC) , 2023
Best Paper of Session Award
paper

We develop a simulation-based framework for evaluating the safety and capacity implications of integrating urban air mobility operations with conventional airport traffic.

Toward Autonomous Air Traffic Control

My long-term research goal is to develop autonomous systems that can reason about complex multi-aircraft situations, anticipate future traffic evolution, and make reliable high-level decisions under uncertainty. I am particularly interested in combining multi-agent trajectory representations with generative models, large language and vision models, and structured decision-making methods.

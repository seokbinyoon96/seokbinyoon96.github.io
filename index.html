Seokbin Yoon

I'm a first-year Ph.D. student in Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering at George Washington University, where I am a member of the Intelligent Aerospace Systems Lab, advised by Peng Wei. My work draws on machine learning and air traffic management to develop data-driven methods for understanding, modeling, and supporting complex air traffic operations. My research includes multi-agent trajectory modeling, generative modeling of air traffic scenes, and learning-based decision support for air traffic control. Previously, I received my Master's degree in Air Transportation at Korea Aerospace University, advised by Keumjin Lee, where I worked on multi-agent trajectory prediction and generative models for flight trajectories. I received my Bachelor's degree in Air Transportation at the same university.

Research Keywords: autonomous air traffic control, multi-agent systems, human-machine interactions, deep generative models

Research

I'm interested in autonomous air traffic control, multi-agent systems, human-machine interactions, and deep generative models.

Seokbin Yoon

I'm a Ph.D. student in Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering at George Washington University (GWU), advised by Prof. Peng Wei. My research lies at the intersection of air traffic control and machine learning, with a focus on developing intelligent and autonomous systems for air traffic management. I received my M.S. and B.S. in Air Transportation from Korea Aerospace University (KAU).

Research Keywords: air traffic management, autonomous air traffic control, trajectory prediction, generative models, multi-agent systems, large language and vision models

Timeline

Currently, I am a Ph.D. student at George Washington University, advised by Prof. Peng Wei. My current research focuses on machine learning and autonomous systems for air traffic control, including multi-agent trajectory modeling, generative models, and learning-based decision making.

Before joining GWU, I received my M.S. in Air Transportation from Korea Aerospace University, where I was advised by Prof. Keumjin Lee. During my master's studies, I worked on multi-agent aircraft trajectory prediction, probabilistic prediction, generative modeling, explainable machine learning, and automatic speech recognition for air traffic control. My research increasingly focused on learning representations of interactions among multiple aircraft and using them to understand and predict complex air traffic situations.

I also received my B.S. in Air Transportation from Korea Aerospace University. As an undergraduate, I studied air traffic control and completed professional air traffic controller training. I later obtained an Air Traffic Controller License from the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport of the Republic of Korea. My background in air traffic control has strongly shaped my research interests in building learning-based systems that can understand, predict, and ultimately support decision making in complex air traffic environments.
